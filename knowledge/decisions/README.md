# Knowledge decisions

- **Purpose:** record evidence-backed decisions within the knowledge model.
- **Accepted:** Markdown decision records with rationale and schema-valid front matter.
- **Prohibited:** architectural or repository-governance decisions (use `project/decisions/`), unapproved commitments, and unsupported rationale.
- **Identifier prefix:** `DECISION`; use `DECISION-NNNNNN` and never reuse an ID.
- **Template:** `decision-template.md` (`DECISION-000000` is not production).
- **Evidence requirement:** at least one valid `EVID` reference.
- **Review requirement:** AI output starts as `draft` or `needs_review`; a decision is not organizationally effective without an authorized human approval record.
- **Upstream inputs:** evidence, concepts, assumptions, risks, and alternatives.
- **Downstream consumers:** relationships, assessments, outlook commitments, project records, and publications.

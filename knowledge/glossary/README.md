# Glossary and vocabulary

This directory defines the cross-cutting semantic layer used to keep terminology consistent throughout the AI Adoption Outlook. It is not part of the `people_and_culture` domain.

The canonical structured term inventory is `../../registers/terms.yaml`, validated by `../../schemas/term.schema.yaml`.

## Intended content

- key terms and approved definitions used by the outlook;
- industry terms, acronyms, aliases, and preferred labels;
- manually composed definitions;
- term candidates extracted through an approved source-processing route;
- traceable evidence references and source IDs;
- broader, narrower, and related-term relationships;
- usage guidance for consistent publication language.

## Term lifecycle

1. Register a `candidate` term without treating it as approved language.
2. Record whether its definition is manual, source-extracted, industry-standard, or combined.
3. Preserve source provenance for extracted or combined definitions.
4. Review classification and the proposed definition.
5. Mark the term `approved` before relying on it as canonical language.
6. Retain deprecated or superseded terms for history and relationship resolution.

Document-content analysis has not started. Automatic extraction may begin only after the contributing source has route-specific processing approval.

# Publications

This directory is scaffolded for future audience-specific publication sources
and generated outputs. The audience subdirectories are empty. Review-only
`AUD-000001` is configured for internal Stage 12 assessment review but grants no
publication or external-release authority. No publication identifiers,
templates, lifecycle, assembler, or publication validator are implemented.

Publications are downstream derivatives of approved canonical knowledge,
assessments, and project records. They are not canonical evidence or knowledge.
Publication-ready executive claims and audience release require authorized
human review.

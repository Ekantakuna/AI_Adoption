# Ecosystem and sourcing assessments

This area contains evidence-backed assessments of markets, providers, offerings, due diligence, performance and exit readiness.

- `market-and-capability/` assesses external capability and market options.
- `provider-evaluation/` retains reviewed comparisons against approved criteria.
- `due-diligence/` retains multi-domain review narratives and conditions.
- `performance-and-value/` evaluates active provider and agreement outcomes.
- `exit-readiness/` evaluates portability, continuity and transition preparedness.

Canonical assessment facts remain in the applicable provider, evaluation, due-diligence, performance and exit registers.

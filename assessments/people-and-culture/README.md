# People and culture assessments

This area contains privacy-safe, evidence-backed AI workforce impact, employee readiness, program evaluation and progress assessments.

- `change-baseline/` establishes the initial change context and stakeholder baseline.
- `workforce-impact/` assesses role, task, competency and capacity impacts.
- `employee-readiness/` assesses aggregated readiness dimensions.
- `program-evaluation/` evaluates campaigns and literacy interventions.
- `progress/` retains dated progress and adoption assessments.

Canonical assessment and finding records remain in the applicable YAML registers.

# Maturity assessment

This directory contains derived maturity-assessment reports and comparison views generated from approved maturity framework, model, assessment and finding records.

## Evidence standard

Every maturity-level judgement requires:

- the framework, model and version used;
- applicable dimension, criterion and level identifiers;
- a qualitative argument explaining the judgement;
- quantitative measures where available;
- source and evidence references;
- confidence and known limitations;
- assessment scope and effective date.

Reports must distinguish sourced facts, assessment judgements, assumptions and recommendations. Canonical assessment data remains in the maturity registers.

## Progress monitoring

Progress is evaluated by comparing compatible approved assessments over time. New use-case adoption, business-process transformation and movement towards strategic intent may contribute evidence, but they do not automatically prove a higher maturity level.

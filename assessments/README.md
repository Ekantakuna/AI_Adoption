# Assessments

This directory contains traceable current-state, target-state, gap, and maturity assessment material.

`governance/` separates AI and data governance current-state, target-state, gap and progress assessments. Governance assessments must reference canonical assessment and finding records and preserve their supporting evidence.

`organization/` separates organization and AI value-operating-model current-state, target-state, gap and progress assessments across ICT, Business, Legal and Compliance.

`people-and-culture/` separates change baselines, workforce impacts, employee readiness, campaign and literacy evaluation, and progress assessment with privacy-safe reporting controls.

`strategic-intent/` separates context and baseline, scenarios and simulations, execution progress, outcome evaluation and strategy review.

`decision-environment/` separates current state, target state, gaps, decision quality and learning-effectiveness assessments.

`data/` separates current state, target state, use-case and product readiness, governance and capabilities, quality, metadata and lineage, and observability assessments.

`engineering-platform/` separates current state, target state, architecture and options, observability and operations, AI UX and catalogue-readiness assessments.

`ecosystem-sourcing/` separates market and capability, provider evaluation, due diligence, performance and value, and exit-readiness assessments.

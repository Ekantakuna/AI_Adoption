# AI and data governance

This domain develops a traceable approach for integrated AI and data governance. Its intended outputs are a defined scope, a versioned TO-BE state, an operating model, policy and guidance architecture, lifecycle risks and controls, technology requirements, literacy and change measures, and an evidence-backed implementation pathway.

No company-specific governance conclusion has been approved. Current-state statements require source evidence; target-state statements must remain proposals until reviewed. Source documents may be analyzed only after their classification and processing route are approved.

## Physical structure

- `01-approach-and-scope/` defines boundaries, principles, applicability and governance layers.
- `02-target-state/` assembles the proposed TO-BE characteristics and intended outcomes.
- `03-operating-model/` describes boards, roles, accountability, decisions, escalation and cadence.
- `04-policy-standards-and-how-tos/` organizes the policy and practical-guidance architecture.
- `05-risks-and-controls/` connects lifecycle risks, treatments, controls and assurance evidence.
- `06-tools-and-technology/` defines vendor-neutral software, workflow, integration and automation requirements.
- `07-literacy-and-change/` develops governance literacy, business-champion, communication and adoption measures.
- `08-pathway/` turns approved gaps into sequenced organizational and technical actions.

Assessment evidence is maintained in `assessments/governance/`. Canonical structured records are stored in the governance registers documented in `registers/README.md`. Controlled vocabulary and semantic relationships are defined in `config/governance.yaml`.

## Required evidence chain

1. Catalogue and approve sources for a permitted processing route.
2. Establish evidence-backed AS-IS observations.
3. Collaboratively define the proposed TO-BE profile and assumptions.
4. Compare AS-IS evidence with approved target characteristics.
5. Review and approve findings and gaps.
6. Create actions linked to approved gaps, owners, dependencies, measures and acceptance criteria.
7. Monitor implementation, control effectiveness and progress without overwriting historical assessments.

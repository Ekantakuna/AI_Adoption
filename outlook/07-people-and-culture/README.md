# People and culture

This domain develops the people-centered change system required for responsible AI adoption and scaling. It covers workforce impact, change-management planning, AI awareness, role-based literacy, employee readiness, business champions, stakeholder engagement, responsibilities, progress measurement and reinforcement.

No employee, workforce, readiness or adoption conclusion has been created. Current-state findings require approved evidence and privacy review. Person-level records are prohibited by default; readiness and workforce reporting must use approved aggregated populations.

## Physical structure

- `01-change-strategy-and-scope/` defines the change vision, scope, objectives, assumptions and exclusions.
- `02-workforce-impact/` covers role, task, competency, capacity, process and accountability impacts.
- `03-sponsorship-stakeholders-and-roles/` covers sponsors, owners, managers, HR/L&D, communications and responsibility assignments.
- `04-awareness-campaigns/` develops audience-specific messages, channels, schedules and reach measures.
- `05-ai-literacy-programs/` defines literacy levels, role-based pathways, outcomes, curriculum, practice and evaluation.
- `06-business-champions/` defines how business champions support literacy, discovery, feedback and adoption through organization networks.
- `07-employee-readiness/` covers privacy-safe readiness baselines, dimensions, findings and targeted interventions.
- `08-plan-timeline-and-actions/` maintains phases, actions, milestones, dependencies, owners and acceptance criteria.
- `09-progress-and-reporting/` defines metrics, append-only snapshots, reporting cadence, risks, blockers and decisions required.
- `10-reinforcement-and-improvement/` covers support, resistance, feedback loops, continued practice and sustained adoption.

Assessment material is maintained in `assessments/people-and-culture/`. Canonical records are documented in `registers/README.md`, controlled values and semantic relationships in `config/people-culture.yaml`, and the reusable semantic layer in `knowledge/people-and-culture/README.md`.

## Reporting chain

1. Establish approved workforce-impact and readiness baselines.
2. Define and approve change-plan scope, phases, responsibilities and measures.
3. Link campaigns, literacy programs and champion networks to audiences and outcomes.
4. Execute owned actions against milestones and acceptance criteria.
5. Capture dated, append-only progress snapshots and metric values.
6. Report reach, participation, learning, readiness, adoption, risks, blockers and required decisions.
7. Adapt interventions without overwriting historical evidence.

# Decision environment

This domain develops the environment in which business problems, options, evidence, predictions, simulations, decisions, overrides, outcomes and feedback are represented and managed.

The strategic target for a decision-centric intelligence system is defined in `../01-strategic-intent/03-decision-intelligence-target/`. This domain will later elaborate decision classes, business semantics, choice architecture, interfaces and learning loops after approved evidence analysis.

The sections separate reusable decision design from actual decision records:

- `01-scope-and-principles/` defines boundaries and design principles.
- `02-decision-portfolio-and-types/` describes recurring decision classes and triggers.
- `03-business-context-and-semantics/` describes problem context and controlled business meaning.
- `04-choice-architecture/` describes option generation and evaluation design.
- `05-evidence-prediction-and-simulation/` distinguishes facts, forecasts and modelled inputs.
- `06-human-authority-and-escalation/` describes decision rights, override and escalation.
- `07-orchestration-and-execution/` describes agent, workflow and human coordination.
- `08-observability-outcomes-and-feedback/` describes execution traces and outcome observation.
- `09-learning-loops-and-evolution/` describes controlled improvement from results.
- `10-assessment-roadmap-and-reporting/` describes evaluation and improvement planning.

Canonical structured records live in the decision-environment registers. No current-state conclusion, approved target implementation, actual business decision or source-content finding is created by this foundation.

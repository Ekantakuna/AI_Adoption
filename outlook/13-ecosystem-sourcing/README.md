# Ecosystem and sourcing

This domain manages the external AI capability and provider lifecycle from an approved partnership need through discovery, evaluation, due diligence, agreement, onboarding, performance, renewal and exit. It covers cloud, frontier models, AI platforms, software, data, advisory, integration, managed services, research, learning and assurance ecosystems.

No provider, offering, sourcing recommendation, agreement or performance conclusion has been created. Provider claims require approved evidence, evaluations require approved criteria, selection requires a referenced decision, and restricted contract content must remain outside this repository.

## Physical structure

- `01-sourcing-strategy-and-scope/` defines principles, boundaries, decision rights and sourcing lifecycle.
- `02-requirements-and-sourcing-cases/` converts partnership and capability needs into controlled sourcing cases.
- `03-market-providers-and-offerings/` structures market discovery and provider or offering metadata.
- `04-evaluation-and-selection/` compares candidates against approved, weighted and evidenced criteria.
- `05-due-diligence/` coordinates legal, compliance, security, privacy, financial, operational and responsible-AI review.
- `06-agreements-and-obligations/` maintains repository-safe agreement, obligation, service-level and control metadata.
- `07-onboarding-and-integration/` covers solution, data, identity, process, support and operating-model integration.
- `08-performance-value-and-renewal/` monitors service, cost, value, risk, roadmap and renewal signals.
- `09-dependency-continuity-and-exit/` manages concentration, lock-in, portability, fallback, transition and exit readiness.
- `10-roadmap-and-reporting/` coordinates sourcing actions, milestones, metrics, issues and decisions.

Assessment material is maintained in `assessments/ecosystem-sourcing/`. Canonical records are documented in `registers/README.md`, controlled values in `config/ecosystem-sourcing.yaml`, and semantic relationships in `knowledge/ecosystem-sourcing/README.md`.

## Lifecycle handoff

1. Organization identifies a capability or capacity gap and creates a partnership requirement.
2. Strategic intent supplies the applicable make, buy or partner choice.
3. Ecosystem sourcing opens and approves a sourcing case.
4. Candidate providers and offerings are discovered and evaluated.
5. Due-diligence functions record independent outcomes, conditions, risks and controls.
6. A separately approved decision identifies the selected provider or offering.
7. Repository-safe agreement metadata links obligations to solutions, controls and metrics.
8. Performance and value are reviewed on a defined cadence.
9. Renewal, recompetition or exit is informed by evidence and an executable exit plan.

# Organization and AI value operating model

This domain develops a traceable organizational vision and operating model for creating Generative AI and broader AI value at scale across ICT, Business, Legal and Compliance. Organization is treated as a system of structure, leadership, accountability, practices, skills, collaboration networks, tools, partnerships and value flows—not only an organization chart.

No company-specific AS-IS drawback, TO-BE structure, named appointment, partner choice or tool selection has been approved. Current-state statements require evidence; target-state recommendations require explicit assumptions, rationale and human review. Source documents remain inaccessible until classification and processing-route approval.

## Physical structure

- `01-vision-and-principles/` defines the organizational vision and design principles.
- `02-current-state/` describes evidence-backed AS-IS composition, practices, interfaces and drawbacks.
- `03-target-operating-model/` assembles the TO-BE structure, accountabilities, decision rights and value flow.
- `04-ai-leadership-team-and-coe/` covers AI leadership, the initial AI team and possible center-of-excellence patterns.
- `05-community-and-champions/` covers communities of practice, functional champions and cross-company knowledge flow.
- `06-practices-and-value-flow/` covers task management, prioritization, product practices, delivery and value realization.
- `07-roles-and-competencies/` defines role purposes, capacity and competency requirements without naming people.
- `08-partnerships/` identifies capability-based partnership requirements without selecting providers.
- `09-tools-and-enablement/` defines vendor-neutral collaboration, work, knowledge, capacity and portfolio-tool requirements.
- `10-pathway/` turns approved organizational gaps into sequenced changes and actions.

Assessment narratives are maintained under `assessments/organization/`. Canonical records are documented in `registers/README.md`, controlled vocabulary and semantic relationships in `config/organization.yaml`, and the reusable semantic model in `knowledge/organization/README.md`.

## Required evidence chain

1. Catalogue and approve organization sources for a permitted processing route.
2. Establish evidence-backed AS-IS units, roles, practices, interfaces, strengths and drawbacks.
3. Collaboratively define the organizational vision, principles and proposed TO-BE profile.
4. Compare current evidence with reviewed target characteristics.
5. Approve findings and gaps before creating pathway actions.
6. Link each action to affected constituencies, roles, units, practices, tools, partners and other AI-adoption domains.
7. Monitor implementation and value-flow improvement through dated reassessments and metrics.

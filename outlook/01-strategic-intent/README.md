# Strategic intent and AI transformation strategy

This domain develops a versioned Generative-AI-focused strategy for transforming how the business frames problems, creates and evaluates options, makes decisions, learns from outcomes and adapts execution. Success is defined through business and decision outcomes rather than the number of models or tools deployed.

The current transformation thesis and five decision-intelligence properties are provisional user direction. They are not evidence-derived strategy conclusions. Source analysis, strategy approval, investment choices and execution commitments have not started.

## Physical structure

- `01-purpose-ambition-and-vision/` defines purpose, ambition and desired AI-adoption state.
- `02-transformation-thesis-and-business-problems/` defines how AI should change problem framing, choices and business operation.
- `03-decision-intelligence-target/` defines the intelligent choice architecture and its required properties.
- `04-strategic-choices-and-hypotheses/` records alternatives, trade-offs, assumptions, causal hypotheses and falsification criteria.
- `05-scenarios-and-simulations/` supports strategy challenge, uncertainty exploration and reproducible simulation runs.
- `06-objectives-and-outcomes/` connects business problems to measurable strategic objectives and outcomes.
- `07-success-factors-metrics-and-indicators/` defines success conditions, outcome measures, leading warnings and guardrails.
- `08-prerequisites-and-domain-alignment/` maps required capabilities and dependencies across the AI-adoption domains.
- `09-execution-plan/` links objectives to initiatives, milestones, dependencies, ownership and intervention rules.
- `10-monitoring-adaptation-and-evolution/` retains assessments, findings, review triggers and immutable strategy-change history.

Assessment material is maintained in `assessments/strategic-intent/`. Canonical records are documented in `registers/README.md`, controlled concepts in `config/strategic-intent.yaml`, and semantic relationships in `knowledge/strategic-intent/README.md`.

## Strategy lifecycle

1. Establish approved evidence about context, current state, decision environment and prerequisites.
2. Formalize the vision, transformation thesis and business problems.
3. Define challengeable choices, assumptions and testable hypotheses.
4. Explore alternatives through scenarios and simulations whose outputs remain non-factual.
5. Approve objectives, success factors, outcomes, indicators and guardrails.
6. Approve a versioned execution plan with owners, milestones, dependencies and review triggers.
7. Assess progress and emerging signals on a defined cadence.
8. Intervene, replan or evolve strategy through traceable change events.

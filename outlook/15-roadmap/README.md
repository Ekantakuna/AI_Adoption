# Roadmap

This folder contains the maturity-improvement pathway associated with the unified `maturity_execution` taxonomy domain. Assessment material is maintained in `../14-maturity/`.

Every pathway action must reference an approved maturity gap or finding, state the intended target outcome, and link to applicable initiatives, milestones, dependencies, metrics and owners. Actions remain proposals until reviewed and approved.

Governance pathway actions are maintained separately in `registers/governance-actions.yaml` and developed in `../08-governance/08-pathway/`. They must reference an approved governance finding and target profile and identify both organizational and technical measures where applicable.

Organization pathway actions are maintained in `registers/organization-actions.yaml` and developed in `../06-organization/10-pathway/`. They must reference an approved organization finding and target profile and identify cross-domain dependencies.

People-and-culture change actions are maintained in `registers/ai-change-actions.yaml` and coordinated through versioned change plans. Their progress is retained in append-only `people-change-snapshots.yaml` records.

The top-level AI strategy execution logic is maintained in `registers/strategy-execution-plans.yaml`. It coordinates cross-domain initiatives and actions but does not replace the detailed pathways owned by each domain.

Ecosystem-sourcing actions are developed in `../13-ecosystem-sourcing/10-roadmap-and-reporting/` and link sourcing cases, provider remediation, agreement milestones, renewal decisions and exit workstreams to shared initiatives and dependencies.
